## Recall (California state law, city elections)
- The state Elections Code recall procedure applies where a city charter is silent. Source: Stockton municipal code language quoted in search results; the state law itself is at https://ballotpedia.org/California_Recall_Law (SECONDARY).
- Signature thresholds depend on city size and the number of registered voters. Ballotpedia's California City entry describes a 25% threshold for a city in the 1,000 to 10,000 voter band. SECONDARY, verify the exact figure for Lake Elsinore's registered-voter count.
- Timing: a recall petition may not be filed when the official's term ends within six months (San Diego rule, example of a city code). Verify Lake Elsinore's code.
- Once a petition is certified, the council must call the election (appellate case law, 1919). SECONDARY.
