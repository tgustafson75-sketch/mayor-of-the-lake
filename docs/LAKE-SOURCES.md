# Lake sources

Mechanics the lake model may use. Each has a source and an "as of" date (2026-11-01 unless noted). Anything not listed here must not appear in the model.

## Documented mechanics
1. **Nutrient loading drives the problem.** Nitrogen and phosphorus pollution is the principal cause of poor water quality. Source: SAWPA, Lake Elsinore and Canyon Lake TMDL Task Force. https://sawpa.gov/?p=757 . Also Santa Ana Regional Water Board 2019 draft resolution: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/santaana/water_issues/programs/tmdl/docs/elsinore/2019/Tentative_Resolution_March_15_2019.pdf
2. **Algae blooms and low dissolved oxygen cause fish kills.** Documented since the late 1800s and early 1900s. Same 2019 resolution.
3. **Targets.** Summer average chlorophyll-a no greater than 40 µg/L (interim, 2015). Depth-average dissolved oxygen no less than 5 mg/L. Source: 2004 TMDL as summarised in the 2019 resolution, above.
4. **Nutrient limits were tightened in July 2025** by the Santa Ana Regional Water Board. https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/press_room/press_releases/2025/pr20250725-santa-ana-tmdls.html
5. **Lake level goal: about 1,240 to 1,247 ft above sea level.** Source: LESJWA, Lake Elsinore Stabilization and Enhancement Project, Final PEIR (2005). https://www.sawpa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/16-Lake-Elsinore-Final-PEIR-MWH-9-05.pdf . A city official later said the aim was about 1,240 ft.
6. **Management options:** in-lake aeration (compressed-air system, LESJWA project record, https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2002081110 ), dredging, and alum treatment (SAWPA 2013 handouts, https://www.sawpa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/2013-7-31-Handouts1.pdf ).
